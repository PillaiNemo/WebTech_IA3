How It Works

Report. Track. See the Change. Here is what happens to a complaint from the moment you send it.

Four steps

  1. Report a problem

    Choose the category, describe it, add a photo and pick the location.

    Before you submit, CivicFix looks for open issues of the same kind within 150 metres. If one matches, you can add your support instead of creating a duplicate. Your name and phone are optional.

  2. Track progress

    You get a reference ID such as CF-2026-00123.

    Enter the ID on the Track Complaint page any time. Every change of status is added to the timeline with the date and the official's note.

  3. Authorities act

    Your report reaches the right department.

    Officials move the issue to Acknowledged and In Progress, add notes, and merge duplicates of the same problem into one main issue.

  4. See the resolution

    Track the issue until it's resolved.

    If a resolved issue isn't really fixed, press "Still not fixed". When three different people do this, it reopens automatically.
