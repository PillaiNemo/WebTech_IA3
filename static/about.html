About CivicFix

Every street has a pothole someone has complained about three times. CivicFix makes those complaints public, counts the days they are ignored, and keeps them on the record until they are fixed.

Our mission

Civic problems rarely go unnoticed; they go unrecorded. A phone call to the ward office leaves no trace, and the next caller starts from zero. CivicFix gives every problem a public ID, a photo, a map pin and a clock that keeps counting.

When many residents report the same thing, we do not create a pile of duplicates. We turn them into one strong issue that shows how many people are affected, which pushes it up the priority list.

CivicFix is a college Web Technology project. It is built only with HTML, CSS, Bootstrap, JavaScript, JSON, jQuery, React, Node.js, Express and MongoDB, and every part of it is explained in the project's viva cheat-sheet.

What we believe

Click a card to read more.

◎

Public by default

Anyone can see an issue and its full history.

No logins to read anything. Officials see the same timeline the public sees, which keeps everyone honest.

⧉

One issue, many voices

Duplicates become support, not noise.

Before you submit, we show similar issues within 150 metres. "Add my support" makes the existing report stronger.

◷

Time matters

Every open issue shows its days ignored.

After 30 days an issue is marked Escalated. If a "fixed" issue isn't really fixed, three residents can reopen it.

Our journey

  1. The idea: one street, five complaints about the same pothole, none of them recorded.
  2. The design: a calm, paper-like interface that anyone can use in under a minute.
  3. The rules: one vote per person, duplicates merged, nothing hidden without three flags.
  4. Today: reports from 10 states, tracked publicly until they are fixed.
